Data & feeds

Everything on this calendar is open for any website, screen or app to read. Files update every morning.

  • api/today.json — today's Hebrew date, yahrzeits and history
  • api/tomorrow.json — tomorrow (the Hebrew date that begins tonight)
  • api/day/<YYYY-MM-DD>.json — any civil date, about 14 months ahead
  • api/date/<day>-<Month>.json — one Hebrew date, e.g. 15-Cheshvan, 7-Adar, 14-Adar-II
  • api/all.json — the full calendar (yahrzeits + history)
  • api/yahrzeits.json · api/history.json
  • api/yahrzeits.csv · api/history.csv — for Excel / Sheets
  • api/calendar.ics — subscribe in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar
  • api/status.json — last build time and counts

Month names: Tishrei, Cheshvan, Kislev, Teves, Shevat, Adar, Adar-I, Adar-II, Nisan, Iyar, Sivan, Tammuz, Av, Elul. The Hebrew date begins at nightfall the evening before the civil date.

Every name is checked against two or more independent sources. Single-source names are kept apart in api/unconfirmed.json and are not shown on the calendar.
